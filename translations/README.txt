LEXIO - TRANSLATION FILE

File: lexio_strings_translate.csv  (UTF-8, 183 strings, ~5,800 source characters)

Columns
  key            do not change
  en (source)    translate FROM this
  tr (reference) Turkish version, only to understand the intended meaning
  de / fr / es / it   fill in (leave a column empty if you don't want that language)
  notes          hints for that row

Rules for translators
  1. Keep placeholders exactly as they are: %1$d  %2$d  %1$s  %3$d ...  They are replaced by numbers/names
     at runtime. They may move within the sentence but must not be changed or removed.
  2. Keep "\n" (line break marker) where it appears.
  3. Keep emoji.
  4. Keep the brand and product terms: Lexio. Keep original legal terms when in doubt.
  5. Tone: friendly, informal, second person ("du", "tu", "tú", "tu"), like a study app for law students.
  6. Short strings are buttons and labels: keep them short (they must fit on a phone screen).
  7. Rows marked "PRIORITY (notification)" are the daily reminder notification and its permission dialog.
  8. Return the SAME csv file with the language columns filled in. Do not add or reorder rows or columns.
